# Forms

`wh347-rev2025.pdf` is the U.S. Department of Labor's Davis-Bacon and Related Acts Weekly
Certified Payroll Form, WH-347 (Rev. January 2025, OMB 1235-0008). It is a U.S. government
work in the public domain. Wagebench uses it to read the text layer of payrolls filled on the
official form and to generate demonstration payrolls. The form states it is for the
contractor's optional use; any format with the same information and an identically worded
Statement of Compliance is acceptable.
